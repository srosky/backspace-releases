<h1 align="center">Backspace</h1>

<p align="center">A quiet count of every backspace, right in your Mac's menu bar.</p>

Backspace shows how many times you've pressed ⌫ today and resets at a time you choose. Click the count for
yesterday, your 7-day average and a 14-day chart. Sounds, a daily limit and notifications are all optional.

**Private by design:** only counts are stored, never what you type, and nothing leaves your Mac apart from a daily
update check. Signed by Northerly Labs Ltd. and notarized by Apple.

**Requirements:** macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.

### Install

1. Download `Backspace.zip`, open it, and drag **Backspace** into **Applications**.
2. Open Backspace and click **Grant Access**, then turn on **Backspace** in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Input Monitoring.
3. `⌫ 0` appears in your menu bar and starts counting.
